📊 PRODUCT DATA FORMAT:
========================

Each product in products-data.json:

```json
{
  "id": 1,
  "name": "Product Name",
  "price": "2500",
  "category": "healing",
  "badge": "NEW ARRIVAL",
  "description": "Product description...",
  "benefits": ["Benefit 1", "Benefit 2"],
  "image": "data:image/... or URL",
  "createdAt": "2026-02-16T00:00:00"
}
```

Categories:
- healing, love, protection, abundance, clarity, chakra

Badges:
- NEW ARRIVAL, BESTSELLER, POPULAR, LIMITED, or empty ""
